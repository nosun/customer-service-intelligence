# 退货与退款处理指南

供客服同事日常参考与讨论 · 2026 年 10 月

**处理目标：先理解客户的问题，再给出清楚、可执行的解决办法。每次回复都说明当前进展，以及下一步由谁做什么。**

本文根据实际邮件往来整理。退货期限、费用、退款比例和特殊补偿，以对应站点适用政策及本案批准结果为准。

## 一、日常处理流程

### 1. 看清客户要解决什么

先读完整往来，区分普通不合身、疑似尺寸做错、到货迟到、破损或错发。同一位客户可能同时遇到几个问题，回复时都要照顾到。

核对订单、具体商品、颜色、尺码、签收日期和首次申请日期，留意之前已经承诺过什么。客户已经提供的信息，不要重复询问。

### 2. 只确认必要条件

普通不合身，通常先确认衣服是否仅试穿、未洗、未改及当前状态，不默认要求身体和衣服测量照片。客户认为制作尺寸有偏差时，再针对相关部位索取测量资料。

破损问题先查看已有照片；需要视频时询问是否保留，没有视频则继续评估其他证据。到货就有的损坏、到货时没有封签，都要单独记录，不能直接当作客户造成。

### 3. 提供清楚、可行的方案

根据政策和授权，可考虑退回退款、保留商品补偿、本地捐赠后退款，或特殊批准的免退回退款。无需每次都提供全部选项。

每个方案说清四件事：**退多少钱、包含哪些费用、客户需要做什么、满足什么条件后办理。** 涉及跨国退回时，说明目的地及运费承担，尽早评估邮费是否合理。

### 4. 确认客户真正接受了什么

“我接受退款”与“只有停止剩余分期我才接受”不同。带条件的回复要先澄清，不能直接当作同意。

更换方案时说明新旧关系。客户接受替代方案后，记录旧方案不再继续，避免重复退款或同时要求客户退回、捐赠。

### 5. 发出完整指引，跟进结果

退回前确认商品和收件地址，提供匹配的表格、发票、运输要求及寄出期限。资料未齐时，不要催客户先寄。

捐赠方案提前讲清机构和凭证要求；凭证审核合格后，不再重复索证。邮费过高、无法承运或资料确实无法取得时，重新评估方案，并约定下一次跟进。

### 6. 核对后办理退款

核对约定金额、履行条件、原交易、已有退款及争议记录，再由有权限的同事办理。

**“客户已接受”“我们将办理”“已向支付渠道提交”“客户已到账”是不同状态。** 邮件只说明已经确认的状态。PayPal、Afterpay 等分期安排由支付方调整，不能承诺部分退款后所有剩余分期都会停止。

## 二、几个容易出问题的地方

- 已及时申请的客户，不因我们内部调查耗时而被重新认定为逾期。
- 约定的固定净退款，不再额外扣一次未说明的费用；但也不等于原运费另行退还。
- 商品描述对不上，先核实再发退货单据；已确认的其他内容不必反复询问。
- 已安排的物流跟进要继续推进，不只把问题交给客户自己找承运商。
- 缺少最终结论时，可以先安抚、说明调查进展和下一步，不必等所有问题解决才回复。

## 三、邮件模板

将方括号内容替换后使用，删除不适用的段落。姓名、站点和签名按实际填写；特殊方案及费用条件应先确认。没有依据的处理时限，不从其他邮件照搬。

### 1. 首次受理：普通不合身

适用：已找到订单，仍需确认商品状态；客户若已回答，直接进入下一步。

```text
Dear [First name],

I’m sorry that the dress did not fit as you hoped. I understand how disappointing that can be, and I’d like to help you with your return request.

For the [dress name] in order [order number], could you please confirm that, apart from trying it on, the dress has not been worn, washed or altered? Please also let us know whether it remains in the condition in which you received it.

Once we have these details, we can confirm the available options and explain the next steps. Please wait for our approved return instructions before sending the dress back, so we can make sure it reaches the correct location with the necessary paperwork.

Thank you for your help.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 2. 条件已确认：提供退回与保留商品方案

适用：两种方案及费用均已获批准。若只有一种可用，只保留对应选项。以下费用写法仅适用于原运费及保障费不退、客户承担退运费的个案。

```text
Dear [First name],

Thank you for confirming the condition of the dress. We can offer the following options for the [dress name] in order [order number]:

1. Return the dress — refund [currency and net amount]
You can return the dress to our designated location in [city, country]. The refund will be processed after the returned dress has been received and checked against the agreed return conditions. You would arrange and pay for the return shipping. The original shipping charge and shipping protection are not included in this refund, and no further restocking fee will be deducted from the amount quoted above.

2. Keep the dress — partial refund [currency and amount]
If you would prefer to keep the dress, we can offer this partial refund with no return required. The original shipping charge and shipping protection would not be refunded separately.

Please let us know which option you prefer. If you choose to return the dress, we will send the final address and required documents. Please wait for those instructions before mailing it.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 3. 客户选择退回：发送正式指引

适用：商品、地址、文件、金额和寄出期限已全部核对。附件需实际附上；费用或验收条件不得在此时新增。

```text
Dear [First name],

Thank you for confirming that you would like to return the [dress name] in order [order number]. The agreed refund is [currency and net amount], subject to the return conditions we have already discussed. No additional restocking fee will be deducted from this amount.

Please find the return form and required shipping documents attached. The confirmed return address is:

[Recipient name]
[Full street address]
[City, state or province, postal code]
[Country]
[Recipient phone number, if required]

Please include the completed return form inside the parcel and follow the attached instructions for the other documents. Use [approved trackable shipping method] and mail the parcel by [confirmed date and time zone].

Once you have shipped it, please email us the tracking number and keep your postage receipt. Please keep the dress in the agreed return condition while preparing it for shipment.

After the parcel has been received and inspected, we will proceed with the agreed refund and send you a confirmation once it has been issued.

If you have difficulty arranging shipment or completing the paperwork, please contact us before mailing the parcel so we can help clarify the next step.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 4. 退运费过高：提供已批准的本地捐赠方案

适用：捐赠机构范围、证明要求和退款已获批准。寄售需要另行写明接收或售出的触发条件，不直接套用捐赠表述。

```text
Dear [First name],

I’m sorry that the return-shipping quote is [quoted cost]. I understand why that is not practical compared with the refund you would receive.

To avoid international return postage, we can offer an alternative: donate the dress to [approved type of local charity] and receive a [currency and amount] refund. This amount applies to the dress only; the original shipping charge and shipping protection would not be refunded separately.

If you choose this option, please obtain a receipt showing the charity’s name, the date it accepted the dress, a description of the donated item and your name as the donor. We will arrange the agreed refund after reviewing and accepting that documentation.

Please confirm that you would like to use this option before donating the dress. If your chosen charity cannot provide these details, let us know first so we can check an alternative form of confirmation.

Your existing return option remains available under its agreed terms while you consider this alternative. If you accept the donation arrangement, it will replace that option; the refunds cannot be combined.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 5. 客户要求停止分期：先解释再确认

适用：客户把停止分期作为接受条件，支付渠道及拟议退款已核实。

```text
Dear [First name],

Thank you for explaining your concern about the remaining installments. I’d like to clarify how the proposed [currency and amount] refund would work before you decide.

We would issue the refund to your original [payment provider] transaction. Your payment provider determines how that refund is applied to your plan. Depending on the plan and the provider’s rules, it may reduce the outstanding balance, be credited back to your payment method, or be allocated between the two.

We cannot confirm that this refund would cancel all remaining installments. It would not necessarily mean receiving the full refund amount in cash as well as having the remaining balance cleared. Please check the refund allocation with your payment provider and continue to follow its current payment instructions unless it confirms an adjustment.

We understand that your acceptance depended on the installments stopping, so we have not treated your reply as agreement to the revised offer or processed a refund under it.

With this explanation in mind, please let us know whether you would like to accept the proposed refund. If this does not address your concern, please tell us so we can review the remaining options.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 6. 特批方案：免退回退款

适用：已取得免退回退款授权。替代方案和费用范围按本案修改。

```text
Dear [First name],

I understand that the return arrangements have added to your frustration, and I’m sorry this has been difficult.

We would like to offer a [currency and amount] goodwill refund for the [dress name] in order [order number], with no return required. You may keep the dress, and you would not need to donate it, take it to a consignment store or pay return postage.

If you accept, this offer will replace the earlier return and donation options; the amounts cannot be combined. The original shipping charge and shipping protection would not be refunded separately.

The refund would be applied to your original payment transaction. If you paid through an installment plan, your payment provider will determine how it adjusts the balance and any credit to your payment method. We cannot confirm that all remaining installments will stop.

Please let us know whether you accept these terms. No refund under this revised offer has been processed yet.

Thank you for giving us the opportunity to work toward a resolution.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 7. 客户已接受：确认将办理退款

适用：以免退回方案为例，客户明确接受、内部批准且办理条件满足。退回或捐赠方案应按实际结果替换第二段，不得提前说条件已满足。

```text
Dear [First name],

Thank you for confirming. We will proceed with the agreed [currency and amount] refund for the [dress name] in order [order number] to your original payment method.

As agreed, no return or donation is required, and you may keep the dress. This refund replaces the earlier options and will not be combined with them.

We will send you a refund confirmation email once it has been issued. Any time needed for the credit to appear in your account will depend on your payment provider and bank.

Thank you for your patience while we worked through this with you.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 8. 捐赠凭证已审核：确认资料齐全

适用：已经实际查看凭证，确认符合此前约定，且已决定安排退款。

```text
Dear [First name],

Thank you for sending the donation receipt. It identifies you as the donor and confirms that the charity accepted the dress, so the documentation for our agreed arrangement is complete. You do not need to provide another receipt or visit the charity again.

We will proceed with the agreed [currency and amount] refund for the dress in order [order number] to your original payment method. This amount covers the dress only; the original shipping charge and shipping protection are excluded, as previously agreed.

We will email you a confirmation once the refund has been issued. If you used an installment plan, your payment provider will determine how the refund adjusts your plan and any credit to your payment method.

Thank you for your patience and for donating the dress.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 9. 包裹损坏调查：安抚并说明下一步

适用：物流确已联系，尚无法确定损坏环节，本案需要补问开箱视频，并已安排继续跟进。根据客户已有资料删减问题。

```text
Dear [First name],

I’m very sorry that the parcel and dress arrived damaged, and for the frustration caused by our earlier replies. We understand that you are asking for help with the damage as well as a return.

We have contacted our logistics partner. At this stage, they have not been able to determine where in transit the damage occurred. To help with their investigation, do you have a video showing the parcel before it was opened and the unpacking? If you do, please send it to us. If no video is available, please let us know so we can discuss what other evidence may help.

We will continue speaking with our logistics partner about the checks they can carry out. We have also noted your return request. We would like to clarify the circumstances of the damage first so we can assess the appropriate next steps for that request.

Thank you for your patience. We’re sorry for the difficulties you have experienced.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

### 10. 退款已成功发起：发送确认

适用：支付渠道已确认退款成功发起，金额及原交易已核对。仅有内部安排或接口受理、仍在处理中的，不使用此版本。

```text
Dear [First name],

We have issued the agreed refund of [currency and amount] for the [dress name] in order [order number] to your original payment method.

The time needed for the credit to appear will depend on your payment provider and bank. If your purchase was made through an installment plan, please check your provider’s updated plan for how the refund has been applied.

Thank you for your patience throughout this process. Please let us know if you have any questions about the refund.

Warm regards,
[Agent name]
Customer Service
[Site name]
```

## 四、每次跟进留一条简短记录

交接时写清：**客户诉求｜已核实事实｜当前方案及金额｜客户是否接受、是否带条件｜当前办理进度｜下一步、负责人和跟进时间。**

例如：客户因迟到及不合身申请退货；跨国邮费过高；已批准免退回退款 US$115；客户已明确接受；尚未发起退款；由负责同事核对原交易和已有退款后办理，并发送确认。
